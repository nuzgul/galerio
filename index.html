Galerio privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Galerio is a photo and storage cleaner. It works on your phone, offline. This policy explains what happens with your data.

Your photos and videos

Galerio reads your photos and videos only to find duplicates, similar shots, screenshots, large videos and other clutter. All processing happens on your phone. Your photos, videos and their contents are never uploaded, shared or sent anywhere. Nothing is deleted without Android's own confirmation: items go to Android's trash, where you can restore them for 30 days.

What Galerio stores on your phone

To make the next scan fast, Galerio keeps a local cache: file names, folders, sizes, dates, and small visual fingerprints and scores of your photos (not the photos themselves). It also keeps your cleanup history (for Undo), protected albums and items, your settings, your daily free-clean counter and your Pro status. This data stays on your phone and is not included in backups or transfers to a new phone.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Galerio asks for your consent with Google's consent form first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy. Galerio Pro shows no ads. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Purchases

Galerio Pro is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment; Galerio never sees your payment details. Galerio only learns from Google Play whether Pro is active. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Notifications

If you allow it, Galerio shows at most one weekly reminder with the space you can free. It is created on your phone from your last scan; nothing is sent.

What Galerio does not do

No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking of your photos, no sale of data.

Deleting your data

Uninstall Galerio, or open Android Settings → Apps → Galerio → Storage → Clear storage. This removes everything Galerio stored. Items in Android's trash stay there until Android empties it (30 days) or you restore them.

Children

Galerio is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version appears in the app and on the store listing. Questions: use the developer email shown on Galerio's Google Play page.
